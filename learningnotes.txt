Frontmatter is the metadata block placed at the very beginning of a Markdown file, sandwiched between triple dashes (---).

Inside --- (Frontmatter): Machine-readable configuration written in YAML. The parser uses this to extract structured properties like title, author, and tags without displaying them as regular text.

Below --- (Body): The actual Markdown content meant for humans to read, which gets counted for word_count and split during chunking.
